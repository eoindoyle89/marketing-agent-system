# Anti-AI Writing Rules — Quietude (Example)

## Metadata

- Owner: Casey | Status: Approved (baseline adopted 2026-06-16)

Baseline generic-AI-tell rules adopted from the repo template unchanged:
challenge the overused-word list (delve, unlock, elevate, leverage, seamless,
journey, "in today's world"...), kill rule-of-three adjective stacks,
"not just X but Y" patterns, hedge stacking, throat-clearing openers, and
restatement endings.

Company additions:

- Quietude never uses exclamation marks anywhere.
- No second-person commands in headlines ("Discover...", "Transform...").
- Brand-specific banned terms live in `brand-voice.md` and apply on top of
  this baseline; the two lists are checked separately in review.
