# Company Context — Quietude (Example)

## Metadata

- Owner: Alex | Status: Approved | Last updated: 2026-06-14 | Sources: SRC-003, SRC-006

## Identity

Quietude is a nervous system intelligence platform: clinically validated
spatial audio, an AI reflection companion (Mira), eye-mask hardware, venue
installations, and a practitioner network. One sentence from the seed deck:
"We start with sound. We expand to every sense. We end with cities."

## Business Model

B2B seeds the market; D2C harvests. Venue installs create exposure that
converts to app subscriptions; six compound channels (referral, Guides,
content, home hosting, PR, community) are designed to push blended CAC toward
zero over time. Current marketing scope is D2C-led; Alex owns B2B personally.

## Stage And Priorities

Pre-seed-close, mid-raise on a $3M seed. Four-person team. $0 paid-media
budget until the round closes. Priorities: fix first-session activation, ship
lifecycle flows, stake SEO pillars, launch the ambassador program.
