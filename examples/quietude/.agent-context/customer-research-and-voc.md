# Customer Research And Voice Of Customer — Quietude (Example)

## Metadata

- Owner: Casey | Status: Usable with limitations | Last updated: 2026-06-18
- Sources: SRC-007 (42 App Store reviews, 9 interviews) | Confidence: Medium

## Limitation

Small sample, skewed to engaged users. Use for language and objection
patterns; do not use for market-size or percentage claims.

## Verbatim Language (quote permission recorded per item)

- "It's the first thing that works *before* I've calmed down." (interview 3,
  quote approved, anonymous only)
- "I don't have to be good at it." (interview 7, quote approved, anonymous)
- "Meditation felt like homework. This feels like a bath." (review excerpt,
  paraphrase only — no verbatim permission)

## Jobs, Pains, Triggers

- Job: down-regulate on demand — after work, before sleep, mid-spiral.
- Pain: every previous tool required effort exactly when effort was
  impossible.
- Trigger events: sleep collapse during a work crunch; ADHD diagnosis;
  partner complaints about phone-scrolling at night.

## Objections (for content and lifecycle)

1. "Another subscription." 2. "Is this just expensive white noise?"
3. "I failed at Calm/Headspace; I'll fail at this." — answer with the
bottom-up frame, never with feature lists.
