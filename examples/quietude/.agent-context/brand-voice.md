# Brand Voice — Quietude (Example)

## Metadata

- Owner: Alex | Status: Approved | Last updated: 2026-06-16 | Sources: SRC-003, SRC-006
- Overall confidence: High

## Positioning One-Liner

Bottom-up nervous system regulation through clinically validated spatial
audio — for people whose minds won't switch off.

## Voice Attributes

Authoritative yet accessible; intimate yet professional; revolutionary yet
grounded. Authority comes from lived experience and evidence, never from
explaining. Speak from the body, not the mind: language should open space,
not close meaning. The goal of any piece is "something in me moved," not
"I understand this concept."

## Vocabulary

- **Yes:** aliveness, inner life, nervous system, spatial sound, resonance,
  somatic safety, embodied clarity, natural rhythm, orientation.
- **Banned (brand-specific):** zen, chill, vibes, high-vibe, unwind-cliché
  phrasing, meditation clichés, "let me explain why this works", didactic
  explainer tone. (Generic AI tells live in `anti-ai-writing-rules.md`.)

## Lead Frame

Lead with regulation and the bottom-up mechanism contrast. **Never lead with
relaxation-app or meditation framing**, even though rest is a real outcome.
Mechanism term: "nervous system regulation" — it explains; it never headlines.

## CTA Rule

Never pressure. "We do not remind. We invite."

## Tone Shifts

Lifecycle email: warmer, shorter. App Store: concrete and sensory. Founder
LinkedIn: first person, lived experience, evidence held lightly.
