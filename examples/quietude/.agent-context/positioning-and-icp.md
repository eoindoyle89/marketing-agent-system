# Positioning And ICP — Quietude (Example)

## Metadata

- Owner: Alex | Status: Approved | Last updated: 2026-06-14 | Sources: SRC-003, SRC-006, SRC-007

## Category Frame

Quietude claims a category the market hasn't named: **bottom-up nervous
system regulation through spatial audio**. The defining contrast, in Alex's
words (SRC-006): *meditation is top-down; Quietude is bottom-up.* Meditation
asks the mind to command the body and fails the stressed brain it claims to
help. Quietude enters through the brainstem, before the thinking mind.

This frame leads every piece of external content. It is the configured
**lead frame**; wellness-generic "relaxation" language must never lead.

## ICP (D2C)

Overstimulated high-achieving professionals, 25-45, urban. Tech workers,
founders, creators, academics, designers. Often neurodivergent (ADHD, HSP).
Sophisticated wellness buyers who have already tried meditation apps.

- **Stated problem:** "I can't shut my brain off. Meditation apps don't work
  for me." (verbatim cluster, SRC-007)
- **Real problem:** overstimulation, not under-motivation.
- **Buying:** the feeling of stability; effortless effectiveness; a beautiful
  ritual, not another discipline to fail at.

## Anti-Personas

Casual relaxation-app browsers price-shopping free ambient sound; meditation
purists seeking guided practice depth. Do not write for either.

## Alternatives

Do-nothing (scroll to sleep), meditation apps (tried, churned), sleep-sound
apps, white-noise hardware. The status quo is the primary competitor.
