# QA Policy — Quietude (Example)

## Metadata

- Owner: Alex | Status: Approved | Last updated: 2026-06-28

1. Every external asset passes the relevant review gate (LinkedIn posts →
   `linkedin-post-reviewer`; articles → `linkedin-article-reviewer`) before
   human approval.
2. Claims check: every factual statement traces to an Approved or
   Use-With-Boundary proof point, with boundaries intact.
3. Voice check: brand-voice vocabulary rules plus anti-AI baseline.
4. Approval is scoped: a named asset, version, and channel. Approval of an
   article is not approval of its excerpts elsewhere.
5. Approvals are recorded in the relevant file and `changelog.md`
   immediately, not at end of week.
6. Nothing publishes, sends, or spends without Alex or Casey.
