# linkedin-ghostwriter

Writes a finished LinkedIn post from a developed content idea, in a named
person's voice, against a configured brand position. Fill in the configuration
once — who the reader is, who the authors are, what every post must lead with,
what the banned words are — and the skill enforces a seven-step structure on
every draft: a hook with a turn in it, a bridge to the reader's world, a method
they could use this week, the skeptic's objection answered, a one-line thesis,
and a clean ending. No calls to action, no product pitch.

It carries one rule I added after a real failure. If the anchor story sits
outside the author's world, the post either routes to a different author or
opens by owning the gap: "I couldn't tell you the rules of a Home Run Derby.
But this story stopped me." A ghostwritten post that pretends to expertise the
author doesn't have reads as fake to the exact people it's meant to convince,
and that's a worse outcome than not posting.

I use this on every founder-brand post at my company. The daily version is
wired to our positioning docs; this is the generalised copy. Pair it with
[linkedin-post-reviewer](../linkedin-post-reviewer/), which judges what this
one writes.
