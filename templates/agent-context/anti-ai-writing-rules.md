## Purpose

Use this before drafting external-facing copy. These rules catch generic model
tells. Company-specific voice and banned brand terms live in `brand-voice.md`.

## Words To Challenge

delve, unlock, elevate, leverage, robust, seamless, landscape, realm,
testament, journey, game-changer, cutting-edge, in today's world, when it comes
to, it is important to note, it is worth noting, at the end of the day, dive
into, unpack, navigate, foster, holistic, comprehensive, myriad, plethora,
boasts, showcases, underscores, encompasses.

These words are not forbidden in every case. The problem is frequency and lazy
use.

## Sentence-Level Tells

- Rule-of-three adjective stacks.
- Repeated "not just X, but Y" structures.
- Excessive dash-led clauses.
- Hedge stacking.
- Throat-clearing openers.
- Closing paragraphs that only restate the piece.

## Structure-Level Tells

- Bullets used for arguments that should be prose.
- Bold text scattered through paragraphs to fake emphasis.
- Headers on every tiny section.
- Paragraphs all the same length.
- Restating the question before answering it.

## Revision Rules

- Read the draft aloud.
- Cut the first sentence if it is throat-clearing.
- Replace generic claims with one concrete detail.
- Use varied sentence length.
- Keep one point per paragraph.
- Rewrite anything that sounds like generic corporate LinkedIn copy.

## Company Overrides

See `brand-voice.md` for brand-specific words, phrases, dialect, and examples.
