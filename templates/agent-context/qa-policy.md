# QA Policy

## Purpose

Define review states, blocking checks, approval scope, escalation, and
post-publication correction for marketing outputs.

## Output States

| State | Meaning | Permitted action |
|---|---|---|
| Draft | Not reviewed | Internal work only |
| Reviewed | Checks completed, approval pending | Internal circulation only |
| Approved | Named human approved this version for a defined use | Use only inside that approval |
| Published / sent | External action completed | Monitor and retain record |
| Retired | No longer approved | Do not reuse |

## Severity And Stop Rules

| Severity | Meaning | Required action |
|---|---|---|
| Blocker | Unsupported claim, privacy breach, legal/compliance risk, missing required approval, or wrong audience/offer | Stop and escalate |
| Major | Material positioning, proof, voice, accessibility, channel, or factual failure | Revise and re-review |
| Minor | Non-material clarity, consistency, or formatting issue | Fix before approval where practical |

## Required Checks

| Check | Requirement | Evidence / source | Failure severity | Reviewer / approver |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Factual accuracy | Every factual claim traces to approved context | `proof-points.md` and `sources.md` | Blocker |  |
| Positioning and audience | Output matches approved ICP, problem and differentiation | `positioning-and-icp.md` | Major |  |
| Voice and anti-AI | Output follows company voice and writing rules | `brand-voice.md`, `anti-ai-writing-rules.md` | Major |  |
| Offer and pricing | Terms and CTA are current and approved | `offers-pricing-and-packaging.md` | Blocker |  |
| Channel specification | Format, identity, links, cadence and mandatory elements comply | `channel-rules.md` | Major |  |
| Legal and privacy | Consent, confidentiality, data use and required disclaimers comply | Approved policy/source | Blocker |  |
| Asset rights | Images, logos, quotes and customer names are permitted | `visual-identity-and-assets.md`, `proof-points.md` | Blocker |  |
| Accessibility | Language and assets meet applicable accessibility rules | Channel / company policy | Major |  |
| Tracking | Links, UTMs and conversion events are correct | `goals-metrics-and-funnel.md` | Major |  |

## Pre-Publication Checklist

- Confirm the exact final version.
- Confirm all blocker and major issues are resolved.
- Confirm links, destinations, dates, prices, names and tracking.
- Confirm required human approval and its scope.
- Record the approval and external-action owner.

## Publishing Rules

Agents may not publish, send, submit, or externally act without explicit human
approval.
