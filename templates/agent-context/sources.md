## Field Definitions

- `Authority` records who is positioned to know the fact.
- `Approval` records whether the company has approved the information for use.
- `Freshness` records whether the information is current enough for the task.
- `Confidentiality / permission` controls where the source may be used.
- A public company asset is `Observed` until confirmed; public visibility does
  not make its claims approved truth.
